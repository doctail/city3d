## Descrizione
Rome 3D timelapse: watch Rome grow from a village of huts on the Palatine Hill in 753 BC to the city of today — rise to a million people, fall to a few thousand, and rise again.

The Colosseum, the Pantheon and St Peter's are built before your eyes. The Aurelian Walls go up in AD 271, and the city shrinks inside them after the fall of the Western Empire. An earthquake breaks the Colosseum in 1349, Michelangelo's dome rises over the Vatican, and Rome becomes the capital of Italy in 1871.

No narration. Just the city.
