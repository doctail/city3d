## Descrizione
Seville 3D timelapse: watch the evolution of Seville from the Almohad city Ferdinand III conquered in 1248 to the Seville of today, with every real building appearing in the year it was built.

58,696 buildings from Spain's official land registry (Catastro), each with its real construction year, plus the city that is no longer there: the walled medieval town, the mosque that became a cathedral, the Giralda's Renaissance belfry, the port of the Indies and its caravels, the river that was moved west, the walls that came down, the Plaza de España, the Expo 92 island and the towers of today, ending with a night over the Alamillo Bridge.

No narration. Just the city.
