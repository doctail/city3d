## Descrizione
Paris 3D timelapse: watch the evolution of Paris from the walled island of Philippe Augustus in 1190 to the Paris of today, with every building appearing in the year it was built.

239,538 buildings of today's Paris with their construction year (BDNB), plus more than 250,000 that are no longer there, reconstructed from historical maps: the medieval walls, the Bastille, the houses on the bridges, the old Île de la Cité, the Tuileries Palace, the streets Haussmann cut through, the Eiffel Tower rising in 1889, and the fire and rebirth of Notre-Dame, ending with a night over the city.

No narration. Just the city.
