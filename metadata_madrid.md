## Descrizione
Madrid 3D timelapse: watch the evolution of Madrid from a small Castilian town in 1480 to the capital of 2026, with every real building appearing in the year it was built.

120,964 buildings from Spain's official land registry (Catastro), each with its real construction year and number of floors. The old town before the 1800s is reconstructed from the city's historical growth, so you can watch the court of Philip II, the Plaza Mayor, the Ensanche grid, the Gran Vía and the Castellana rise, all the way to the Cuatro Torres.

No narration. Just the city.
