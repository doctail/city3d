## Description (EN)
Valencia's river made the city, flooded it, and was finally moved. Watch 788 years of Valencia grow around the Turia, from the conquest of 1238 to the garden that now fills its old riverbed.

NO AI imagery. Every frame is rendered from open data: from the mid-19th century every building standing today appears in the year the Spanish land registry (Catastro) says it was built. Before 1840 the buildings are an approximate reconstruction on the historic footprint, labeled on screen as "RECONSTRUCTION · APPROXIMATE". Streets and squares opened by demolition appear only after they were opened. Population numbers are estimates until the censuses, and the video says which.

Which city should grow next? Tell me in the comments. And if a building or a monument is missing from your neighborhood, tell me that too: I would rather fix it.
