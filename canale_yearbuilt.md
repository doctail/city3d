## Descrizione del canale (EN, da incollare)
Watch the world's great cities grow, one real building at a time.

Every Year Built timelapse is rendered in 3D from open data: the real footprint, height and construction year of every building, layered over historical maps of how each city looked before today's buildings replaced the old ones. No narration — just the city rising from forest and farmland to skyline, with the dates, the population and the moments that shaped it.

New city every week. Tell us in the comments which one should be next.

Data: municipal open-data portals (e.g. NYC Open Data), U.S. Census TIGER, OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Rendered in Blender.

## Descrizione del primo video (bozza)
From a forested island in 1609 to the skyline of 2026 — watch New York City grow in 3D, one real building at a time.

Every modern building appears in its real construction year (NYC Open Data, 145,000+ footprints). Older neighborhoods that were later demolished are reconstructed from historical maps, so you can see the wooden town of New Amsterdam give way to brownstones, tenements and towers.
