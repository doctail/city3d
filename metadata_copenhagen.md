## Descrizione
Copenhagen 3D timelapse: watch the evolution of Copenhagen from Bishop Absalon's castle in 1167 to the city of today, with every real building appearing in the year it was built.

131,938 buildings from the Danish building register (BBR), each in its real construction year, plus the city that is no longer there: the medieval town inside its walls, the Great Fire of 1728, the fire of 1795, the British bombardment of 1807, the palace of Christiansborg burning and rising again three times, the bridge quarters after the walls came down, and the harbor city of today, all the way to a night over Tivoli.

No narration. Just the city.
