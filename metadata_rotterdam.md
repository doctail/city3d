## Descrizione
Rotterdam 3D timelapse: watch the evolution of Rotterdam from a dam on a muddy stream in 1340 to the skyline of 2026, with every real building appearing in the year it was built.

268,466 building parts from the Dutch national building register, each with its real construction year and measured height. You can watch the medieval town inside its walls, the Water Town of the Golden Age, the docks of the world's busiest port, the bombing of 14 May 1940, when the whole city center disappeared in a single afternoon, and the city that chose to rebuild from scratch, all the way to the towers of the Kop van Zuid.

No narration. Just the city.
